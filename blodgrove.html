GM Background

What's Actually Going On?

Lord Oswin Prym III is not who he says he is. His real name is Oitharan and he is an elf that was once a king of the Woodwose centuries ago. Over the years, Prym has been learning how to become a necromancer and then use his power to destroy the holy knights of the region and establish a new Green Kingdom. Prym has made a chamber under the forest which connects to the manor where you can find his experiments and sacrifices. Prym has been slowly completing sacrifices which require a lot of time and preparation to keep it secret. Recently, Prym conducted an experiment on Everen giving him lycanthropy, so Prym has been more bold with completing his sacrifices as he has been dressing up as a werewolf using a bear pelt to complete the sacrifices. Prym has to use the power from the sacrifices to dispel the megalith structures around the wood called the “strange stones” which work as wards to contain whatever power is in the woods. Once those stones are dispelled, Prym will then attempt to sacrifice his wife as the final sacrifice needed to raise his undead army to kill the settlers that will refuse to folllow him and then attack the nearby armies of the Church of the Saints. There is a wizard named Rhyndon Everbright in the area who moved here 3-4 years ago researching the weird powers detected in the area. He found evidence that Prym was attempting to raise an undead army and confronted him. However, Prym anticipated this, and with the help of some local bandits, attacked and captured Rhyndon and is keeping him captive in his dungeon to draw power from him. Recently, Prym told his wife, Lady Faeleth Prym, what his plan was, arrogantly assuming she would be supportive but she was horrified. She pretended to be supportive but then tried to write a letter to her sister, Ruma. She yelled for her favorite squire, Garen, frantically telling him she needed him to deliver a letter. She was only able to write one line when Prym approached her. Lady Prym crumpled the letter to hide it, but Prym found it. Knowing Garen was on his way up and didn’t want to make him suspicious, Prym put Lady Prym under a charm spell, then put his seal on the letter and had Lady Prym hand it to Garen who then took it to Lady Ruma.

Adventure Hooks

Lady Ruma of the Temple asks for the Adventurers' Help

"My husband is sick. I need your help"

Lady Ruma of the Temple received this letter from a young squire (Garen) who travelled through the night to deliver it. It was from her sister, Lady Faeleth Prym. The squire said that Lady Prym has frantically called down for him to come and deliver the letter as fast as he could. But, when he went upstairs and got the letter from her, she seemed calm standing alongside her husband, Lord Prym, and gently handed the note to him. Still, he came as quickly as her initial urgency demanded.

Lady Ruma has sent multiple priests to Blodgrove to check on the situation, but they have all been turned away except one, which hasn't returned. The ones that have returned have learned that the area has been experiencing regular werewolf attacks.

Lady Ruma reached out to you to ask you if you can check on the situation and offers ownership of a small homestead not too far outside the city to compensate should Lady Prym be proven to be safe. Garen the squire will offer to take the party back to Blodgrove.

He put her under a charm spell and then found the paper, read it, and when the squire arrived, pretended like he was also having him deliver the letter and applied his seal to the envelope)

Oswin Prym III or Oitharain, The Last Green King of the Blodgrove

Oswin Prym III, whose real name is Oitharan, was once known as a Green King who ruled over clans of cannibalistic humans in the region called Woodwose. This kingdom stood for hundreds of years but then fell after a long war with the Vaelish Druid invaders. The Vaelish killed or expelled all of the Woodwose and also thought they killed the Green King Oitharan but were mistaken. Oitharan hid in the forest after the fall of his kingdom and plotted to exact revenge on the Vaelish which ousted him. Oitharan remained in the forest for over a century, terrorizing the Vaelish settlers, conducting magical experiments to increase his power, and corrupting the forest life to his will. Sensing the dark nature of the forest, yet unaware of the the true origin of that nature, the Druids of the region erected and enchanted the megalith structures around the forest, binding the dark energies, and as a result Oitharan, within. When the original Oswin Prym I, an Airnish human, was given these lands by leaders of the Church of the Saints, Oitharan attacked and killed him and took his identity then becoming the new Oswin Prym I. Since elves have significantly longer lives than human, Oitharan, now known as Prym, would fake his own death and pretend to be his son, Oswin Prym II. He then did it again to become Oswin Prym III who he is now.

Oitharan, now known as Lord Prym, had secured the loyalty of Weasel and Bardos in pursuit of his wicked goals. He also has the Baliff of the town under his thumb as he will only betray Prym if it is becomes obvious that Prym is a necromancer or means to kill the villagers. Weasal follows Lord Prym willingly, but Bardos has been turned into a mindless thrall that Lord Prym uses like a familiar. Bardos always has a large smile on his face no matter what he is doing now and as such, is known as the Smiling Man to the village when he is spotted.

Rumors

Rumors that May be Encountered When Approaching Blodgrove

  • The area has been plagued by werewolf attacks for a long time, but recently, sightings have increased
    • Partially False - All "Werewolf" attacks in the area have always been Lord Prym, though there have been sightings of werewolves in the area lately which are real
  • Oswin Prym IV might soon be returning from training in an Outland Kingdom
    • False -Oswin Prym IV is not real and a rumor started by Lord Prym so he can later take his place and pretend that Oswin Prym III dies
  • If the werewolves don't get you, the Woodwose might
    • False - There's actually no more Woodwose in this area
  • The old Green Temple has recently been occupied by some knight from the south and his men
    • True - This knight and his men are the werewolves recently sighted in the area. They are actually trying to help this child that begged for Lycanthropy to be cured of an illness. This group is dedicated to serving The Moon Goddess and protecting those outcast by the Radiant Dawn
  • Father Mouric will be retiring soon
    • True - He has told people many times that he will be stepping down, however, Lord Prym always convinces him to stay
    • Most recently, a young cleric that Lady Ruma had sent to investigate was being considered by Father Mouric for the role but that cleric ended up as a victim of Lord Prym's sacrifice
  • The Wizard that used to live in the area has been gone for almost a year now
    • True - The Wizard, Rhyndon Everbright, moved into the area investigating the weird arcane power, but ended up locked up by Lord Prym a year ago and has been in Prym's dungeon since
  • The Woodwose ruins are haunted
    • Partially True - The ruins are occupied by bandits...mostly. Though, the occasional ghost or undead has been known to appear at times

The Bear Cave and the Deep Cave

Description

Dank, dark, musty cave with a narrow opening but a wide initial room. In the middle of that initial room, there are the remains of a skeleton of a large bear. About 20% of it remains, but from the skull and the size, it is confidently a bear.

Beyond the first room, a narrow passage way leads to a fork.

To the right is a bear den. If it is after dark, there is a 25% chance that there are sleeping bears. If there are no bears, there is a 10% chance that a bear returns while the party is in the cave.

To the left leads to The Deep Cave which is a larger underground cave running west/east with a waterfall on the west end. On the east end, there is a large pile of rocks beyond which is the western edge of Prym's dungeon. There is also a small area with a crack in the rock wall where you can see into Prym's dungeon.

Clues That Can Be Found

  • No other bones in the area
  • Not much evidence of a decomposing flesh
  • A coat button with the Prym family sigil on it
  • If you ask an animal for information, if they are a long lived animal, they may remember that several years ago, someone killed and skinned the bear. Short lived animals will remember the skeleton always having been there. Several humans have come and gone in the cave, but most don't stay long or get attacked by the remaining bears.

The Wizard's Tower

Tower Entrance

Description

The tower is on a small hill and there is a well in front of the entrance on a small hill that rises slightly higher than the entrance

Entrance Lock Puzzle

Entrance

When you enter the tower, there is a winding staircase around the inside of the tower which leads to a ladder and a trap door When you open the trap door, if the lock is engaged, you will be opening a trap door at the bottom of the well outside and appear there. If the lock is disengaged, then you enter the first floor of the tower

The Well

If you look in the well, you find that the well is dry There is a bucket with a rope tied to its handle near the well which appears like it may have been attached to the well in the past There is an inscription which is faded and accompanied by runes Says "All Is Well When Well Is Well"

Solution

If you add water to the well, then the inscription glows and the "lock" on the entrance is opened allowing one to proceed to the first floor.

First Floor

Description

There are some books around of varying topics, none which appears to be particularly valuable or interesting. There are stairs in the room which lead to a stone wall. There are four windows which appear to have different colors of sky and ambient light coming through the curtains. If a character gets close enough to a window to look out of it, they are transported to a new "version" of the room. Each version is the exact same except one thing different.

The Window Puzzle

The South Window

Appears to show the actual outside. You can see the well outside. This window transports you back to the "True" version of the room they originally entered if they have transported to a different version

The East Window

Shows a sky at morning with the sun rising over lush green fields which are not the lands which are outside the tower as can be easily discerned by the lack of mountains. In this room, at the top of the stairs in front of the stone wall, there is a key on a pedestal.

The West Window

Shows a sky with a setting sun over a vast desert. In this room, at the top of the stairs in front of the stone wall, there is a lock on a pedestal.

The North Window

Shows a sky that is dark but with a full moon. You can see a city below you with lights on in some the buildings, but most are dark. In this room, there is a door at the top of the stairs which will not move at all. There is a place to put the lock.

Solution

If the key is taken from the "Morning" room, the lock is taken from the "Sunset" room, and the lock is then put on the door in the "Midnight" room and then unlocked with the key, then the stone wall will disappear in the 'True" room leading you to the Second Floor

Ethereal Butterflies

Level 2 Transmutation

Casting Time: 1 action

Range: Touch

Target: Self or A willing creature you touch

Components: V S M (A butterfly wing)

Duration: Up to 10 minutes

Classes: Druid, Wizard

Yourself or a willing creature you touch shape-shifts, along with everything it's wearing and carrying, into a swarm of ethereal butterflies. The spell ends if the target drops to 0 Hit Points or if it takes a magic actions to end the spell. While in this form, the target's only method of movement is a fly speed of 20 feet and it can hover. The target can enter and occupy the same space as another creature. The target can move through small openings that butterflies can fit through, and it cannot pass through liquids. The target has immunity to all nonmagical damage, and has Advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. The target can't talk or manipulate objects, and any objects it was carrying or holding can't be used while in this form. The target cannot attack or cast spells in this form. Finally, the target can perform the Help action for an attack when it is able to provide visual distraction or obscuration.
Using Higher Level Spell Slots: You can target an additional creature for each spell slot above 2.

Tree Illusion

Level 1 Illusion Spell

Casting Time: Action

Range: 60 feet

Components: V, S, M (a bit of bark)

Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Classes: Druid, Wizard

You create the illusion that you take the form of a tree. The tree's branches can move as if blowing in the wind when appropriate. The tree can be no bigger than a 15-foot cube. If leaves are part of the illusion, sounds of leaves blowing in the wind can accompany the illusion. The illusion is only visual and auditory; it isn't accompanied by any other sounds or any other sensory effects. Since things can pass through it, physical interaction with the tree, as well as witnessing something passing through it, reveals the tree to be an illusion surrounding you. A creature that takes a Study action to examine the image can determine that it is an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the creature can see through the image.
